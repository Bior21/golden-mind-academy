Construction of Golden Mind Academy has officially begun. The site in
Ayilo I Refugee Settlement has been cleared and the structural phase is
underway, starting with the foundation for the main classroom block.

This milestone belongs to everyone who has supported the project so
far — thank you for helping us break ground.
