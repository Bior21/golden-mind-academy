We are making progress! Thanks to our dedicated team on the ground—masons, engineers, and supportive community members—for bringing our second block of three classrooms to fruition. As Epictetus reminds us, “Nothing great is created suddenly.” Golden Mind Academy has continued to take shape, and every classroom we complete brings a vision that began only as an idea closer to reality. Though we still have a lot of work ahead, including our final classroom block and staffroom, it is difficult, if not impossible, to describe the pride we feel when we look at how far we have come. We will continue building, one step at a time.GMA—Nurturing Leaders, Building the Nation
